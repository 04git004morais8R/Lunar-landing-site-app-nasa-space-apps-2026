Click a row to inspect that site. Colour thresholds are illustrative; set them from your mission's real requirements. A * marks a site without terrain data: its horizon is assumed flat, so its results are optimistic.

Positions come from JPL Horizons (precomputed hourly), terrain horizons from LOLA elevation data. The Moon is treated as a sphere and results are interpolated from hourly samples.
